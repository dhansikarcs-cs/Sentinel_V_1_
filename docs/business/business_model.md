# Sentinel — Business Model

> Pricing is a working hypothesis. Deployment pricing scales with
> customisation complexity, clinic requirements, and hardware economics.

---

## Revenue Architecture

**ONE-TIME — Digital Infrastructure**
Customised website + deployment for clinics and patients.
₹9,999–19,999 / $399–799 (customisation-dependent)

**RECURRING B2B — Clinician SaaS + Maintenance**
Dashboard access, system maintenance, support.
₹2,499 + ₹999/month = ₹3,498/month per clinic / $198/month

**RECURRING B2B2C — Patient Subscriptions**
Website access, BYOD, ring/HaaS — tiered per patient.
₹499–1,499/month per patient / $29–79/month

## Pricing Philosophy

The one-time deployment products are **not standardised templates**. Each
deployment configures:

- Clinic branding and patient-facing website
- Appointment/booking system
- Patient onboarding workflows
- Clinician dashboard integration
- Domain/setup assistance
- Sentinel system integration

**₹9,999** — Basic customised deployment
**₹14,999** — Comprehensive website + booking ecosystem
**₹19,999** — Complex clinic launch with deep Sentinel integration

---

## Formal Positioning

> A customisation-led digital-health infrastructure model with recurring SaaS
> and patient subscription revenue. Pricing is currently a working hypothesis
> and varies according to deployment complexity, customisation requirements,
> and hardware economics.
