# The Sentinel System: A Dual-Sided Mental Health Infrastructure

**Background:** Mental health systems globally face a parallel crisis — patients lack continuous support between sessions, while psychologists face burnout from caseload overload. The patient-to-practitioner ratio in India (0.75 psychiatrists per 100,000) makes individual monitoring impossible. Research increasingly shows that combining physiological data (heart rate, stress, sleep) with emotional self-reporting creates a more complete picture of mental state than either alone — yet no accessible platform integrates both.

**The Sentinel Ecosystem** bridges the gap between clinical sessions and daily reality through biometric sensing, AI-driven analysis, and multi-channel communication serving patients, psychologists, and trusted contacts simultaneously. Its tri-directional architecture connects all three stakeholders in real-time with automated crisis escalation, proof-based follow-up, and cross-signal psychophysiological assessment — a combination no existing platform achieves.

**Core Components:** Patients access a self-monitoring portal with biometric trends, AI-summarized journaling (with daily mood locking and emotion classification across 28 GoEmotions labels), and one-tap crisis trigger that also activates automatically when biodata and emotional trends indicate distress. Psychologists receive a triaged queue of only their assigned patients, AI-synthesized clinical notes via a dual-mode engine (warm reflection for patients, clinical OAP format for notes), and structured follow-up grading. Trusted contacts receive crisis notifications with a dedicated acknowledgment page — no login required. The SQLite/PostgreSQL dual-backend database provides transaction-safe storage with 15 tables covering journals, biometrics, bookings, crisis logs, and daily mood entries.

**Crisis Engine:** A three-stage escalation protocol activates when a patient triggers emergency — an immediate siren alerts the environment, a trusted contact is notified at 30 seconds, and helpline escalation occurs at 60 seconds if unacknowledged. The psychologist can acknowledge at any point, freezing the response timer and stopping escalation.

**AI as Assistant, Not Replacement:** The AI never makes clinical decisions — it summarizes, synthesizes, and triages. The custom `sentinel` model (7.2B parameters, therapy-tuned via Ollama) provides empathy-toned patient reflections and structured clinical notes. A TF-IDF emotion classifier over 28 GoEmotions labels detects emotional states like sadness, fear, and nervousness, feeding into every summary. Echo detection prevents the AI from simply repeating the patient's words. For clinics requiring complete data privacy, the model runs entirely locally — no data leaves the premises. For cloud deployments, Groq API provides fast summarization as fallback.

**Scientific Foundation:** Built on psychophysiological integration — combining biometric trends (BPM, stress, SpO₂, sleep, mood) with emotional journal data and emotion-labeled analysis to assess patient state. This dual-signal approach mirrors research showing that physiological markers often precede emotional self-report in detecting deterioration.

**Impact:** Sentinel does not just support patients — it protects clinicians. By automating triage, summarizing data, and providing self-care reminders, it reduces secondary traumatic stress and burnout. The platform requires minimal infrastructure cost, making it deployable in any clinic, school, or community center.
